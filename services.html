Common Questions About Our Services

How are these services different from regular gym diets?

Unlike generic gym diets, our services are clinical and therapeutic. We analyze your blood reports, medical history, and specific conditions like PCOS or Diabetes to create a plan that heals, not just manages weight.

Do you provide recipes for South Indian meals?

Absolutely! AharaX Wellness is rooted in traditional South Indian nutrition. We specialize in making healthy versions of dosas, idlis, and traditional grains like Ragi and Millets.

Can I switch between services mid-way?

Yes. If your health goals evolve (e.g., from weight loss to pregnancy care), our dietitians will adjust your clinical approach and plan accordingly.
